## Who it's for

You've built large, resilient systems. You know tracing, retries, queues, and what breaks at scale. You've used ChatGPT or Claude, but you haven't built a system on top of a model yet. This book is how I'd onboard you. It covers one idea at a time, starting with what a model is and ending with how you know your agent works.

## Skip what you know

If you read a chapter's title and already know what it's about, you probably won't learn much from the chapter. Skip it, or open its Further reading. Those links go much deeper than I care to explain in the book.
